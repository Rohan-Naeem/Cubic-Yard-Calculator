Definition

What Is a Cubic Yard?

A cubic yard is a unit of volume equal to a cube measuring one yard, or three feet, on every side. One cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet, which is why the cubic yard calculator divides your cubic feet result by 27 to convert it. Cubic yards are the standard measurement used by suppliers of gravel, sand, mulch, soil, concrete, and other bulk materials because it describes a large enough volume to price and deliver in bulk loads.

Why It Matters

Why Calculate Cubic Yards?

Knowing the cubic yard volume of a project area lets you compare supplier pricing, request an accurate delivery quantity, and avoid ordering too much or too little bulk material. Whether you're covering a driveway with gravel, filling raised garden beds with soil, or pouring a concrete slab, converting your length, width, and depth measurements into cubic yards gives you a consistent number to work from.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in one cubic yard, since a cubic yard is a cube measuring 3 feet on each side (3 × 3 × 3 = 27).

How do I convert cubic feet to cubic yards?

Divide your cubic feet value by 27 to get cubic yards. For example, 100 cubic feet ÷ 27 = 3.70 cubic yards.

Can I calculate gravel, soil, or concrete with this calculator?

Yes. The calculator works for any bulk material because it calculates volume, not weight. It applies to gravel, sand, soil, mulch, concrete, and other fill materials.
