About Cubic Yard Calculator

A focused, free tool for turning project measurements into a usable volume estimate.

Purpose of This Website

Cubic Yard Calculator exists to help anyone planning a project — homeowner, landscaper, contractor, or student — quickly turn length, width, and depth measurements into a volume figure in cubic yards, cubic feet, and cubic meters. Instead of doing the arithmetic by hand or hunting for the right conversion factors, you can enter your numbers once and get all three units at the same time.

How the Calculator Works

The calculator takes your entered measurements and unit, converts every length to feet internally, multiplies them according to the shape you selected, and then converts the resulting cubic feet figure into cubic yards and cubic meters using standard conversion factors. All calculations run directly in your browser using JavaScript, so results appear instantly without reloading the page.

Who Can Use It

The calculator is built for anyone estimating bulk material volume, including homeowners planning a landscaping project, contractors quoting driveway or concrete work, gardeners filling raised beds, and students learning volume formulas. No account or sign-up is required.

Supported Measurements

You can enter measurements in feet, inches, meters, or centimeters, and the calculator converts between them automatically. Results are always shown in cubic feet, cubic yards, and cubic meters together, along with the calculated area.

Supported Shapes

The calculator supports rectangular and square areas, circular areas, and a custom volume converter for when you already know a volume figure and just need to convert it between cubic feet, cubic yards, and cubic meters.

Why Results Are Estimates

The numbers this calculator produces are mathematically precise for the measurements you enter, but real-world project sites are rarely perfectly uniform. Ground slope, compaction, material settling, and measurement precision can all cause the amount of material you actually need to differ from the calculated volume. Treat the result as a planning estimate, and see the disclaimer for more detail.
