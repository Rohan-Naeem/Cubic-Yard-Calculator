Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about calculating cubic yards and using this calculator.

What is a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is a unit of volume equal to a cube measuring 3 feet on each side. It's the standard unit used by suppliers to price and deliver bulk materials such as gravel, sand, soil, and concrete.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in one cubic yard (3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 ft³).

How do I calculate cubic yards?

Multiply the length, width, and depth of your area, all measured in feet, to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 to get cubic yards. For circular areas, calculate the area with π × radius² first, then multiply by depth.

How do I convert cubic feet to cubic yards?

Divide your cubic feet value by 27. For example, 150 cubic feet ÷ 27 = 5.56 cubic yards.

How do I calculate a circular area?

Measure the diameter across the widest point of the circle, divide it by 2 to get the radius, then apply Area = π × radius². This calculator does that automatically when you select the Circle option and enter a diameter.

Can I use inches in the calculator?

Yes. Select "Inches" from the unit dropdown and the calculator will convert your measurement to feet internally before calculating volume.

How many cubic yards are in a cubic meter?

One cubic meter equals approximately 1.30795062 cubic yards.

Is a cubic yard the same as a ton?

No. A cubic yard measures volume, while a ton measures weight. How much a cubic yard of a given material weighs depends on that material's density, moisture content, and compaction, so the two units are not interchangeable without knowing the specific material's weight-per-volume figure from a supplier.

Can I calculate gravel with this calculator?

Yes. Enter the length, width, and depth of the area you plan to cover with gravel, and the calculator will return the volume in cubic yards, cubic feet, and cubic meters.

Can I calculate soil with this calculator?

Yes. The calculator works the same way for topsoil, garden soil, or fill soil — enter your area's dimensions and desired depth to get a volume estimate.

Can I calculate concrete with this calculator?

Yes. Concrete slabs, footings, and pads can be calculated using the rectangle option by entering length, width, and slab thickness as the depth.

How accurate is a cubic yard calculator?

The calculator performs exact mathematical conversions based on the measurements you enter, so its accuracy depends on how precisely you measure your project area. Actual material needs can still vary due to ground irregularities, compaction, and supplier-specific measuring practices, so treat the result as a planning estimate.
