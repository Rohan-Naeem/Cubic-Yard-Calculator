Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains what happens to information when you use Cubic Yard Calculator. This site is a simple calculation tool, and it is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Calculator Data

The measurements you enter into the calculator — length, width, depth, diameter, unit selections, and any other calculator inputs — are processed entirely locally in your browser using JavaScript. That data is not transmitted to a server, stored in a database, or shared with any third party as part of the calculator's operation.

Information We Do Not Collect

This site does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or payment information, and does not use the calculator to build a profile of you.

Server and Hosting Logs

Like most websites, the server or hosting provider that delivers these pages to your browser may automatically log standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, for security and operational purposes. This is separate from the calculator itself.

Cookies

This site does not currently set its own cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what cookies are used and why.

Third-Party Services

This site does not currently use analytics, advertising networks, or embedded third-party forms. If any of these are added in the future — for example, an analytics tool to understand site traffic, or an advertising service — this privacy policy will be updated to disclose what data those services collect and how it is used.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the site's functionality changes, such as adding analytics, advertising, cookies, contact forms, or other third-party services. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact information provided elsewhere on this site, if available.
