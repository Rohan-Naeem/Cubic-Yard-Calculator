Cubic Yard Calculator Formula — How to Calculate Volume

The formulas behind the calculator, explained step by step for rectangular and circular project areas.

Rectangle or Square Volume

For a rectangular or square area, volume is length multiplied by width multiplied by depth. All three measurements should be in the same unit, typically feet, before multiplying.

Step 1 — Volume in cubic feet

Volume (ft³) = Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (ft)

Step 2 — Convert to cubic yards

Cubic Yards = Cubic Feet ÷ 27

Worked example — rectangular garden bed

  1. Length = 10 ft, Width = 6 ft, Depth = 6 in
  2. Convert depth: 6 ÷ 12 = 0.5 ft
  3. Volume = 10 × 6 × 0.5 = 30 ft³
  4. Cubic yards = 30 ÷ 27 = 1.11 yd³

Circular Area Volume

For a circular area, first calculate the area using the radius, then multiply by depth to get volume.

Step 1 — Area of the circle

Area (ft²) = π × Radius (ft)²

Step 2 — Volume in cubic feet

Volume (ft³) = Area × Depth (ft)

Step 3 — Convert to cubic yards

Cubic Yards = Volume in ft³ ÷ 27

Worked example — circular flower bed

  1. Diameter = 8 ft, so Radius = 4 ft
  2. Depth = 4 in, converted: 4 ÷ 12 = 0.333 ft
  3. Area = π × 4² = 50.27 ft²
  4. Volume = 50.27 × 0.333 = 16.74 ft³
  5. Cubic yards = 16.74 ÷ 27 = 0.62 yd³

Converting to Cubic Meters

To express a volume in cubic meters instead of cubic yards, divide the cubic feet value by 35.3146667.

Cubic Meters = Cubic Feet ÷ 35.3146667

Worked example — driveway base

  1. Length = 30 ft, Width = 10 ft, Depth = 4 in (0.333 ft)
  2. Volume = 30 × 10 × 0.333 = 99.9 ft³
  3. Cubic yards = 99.9 ÷ 27 = 3.70 yd³
  4. Cubic meters = 99.9 ÷ 35.3146667 = 2.83 m³

Length Conversions Used Internally

The calculator converts every length input to feet before running the volume formula, then converts the result to cubic yards and cubic meters. It uses the following conversion factors:

  • 1 foot = 12 inches
  • 1 foot = 0.3048 meters
  • 1 meter = 3.280839895 feet
  • 1 centimeter = 0.032808399 feet
  • 1 inch = 0.0833333333 feet
  • 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet
  • 1 cubic meter = 35.3146667 cubic feet
  • 1 cubic meter = 1.30795062 cubic yards

Calculations are performed internally using full precision, and results are rounded only when displayed, so small rounding errors do not compound through the process.
