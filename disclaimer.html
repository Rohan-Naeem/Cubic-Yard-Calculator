Please read before relying on calculator results for a project.

Estimates Only

Cubic Yard Calculator provides volume estimates based on the measurements you enter. Results are mathematically accurate for the numbers provided, but they are estimates of your project's needs, not guaranteed quantities.

Measurement Accuracy

The accuracy of any result depends entirely on the accuracy of the length, width, depth, or diameter you measure and enter. Measuring errors, uneven ground, or approximate measurements will carry through to the calculated result.

Actual Project Conditions Vary

Real project sites are rarely perfectly flat or uniform. Slopes, obstructions, existing structures, and irregular boundaries can all cause the actual volume of material needed to differ from a calculation based on simple length, width, and depth.

Material Density Varies

This calculator estimates volume, not weight. The weight of a given volume of material — gravel, sand, soil, mulch, or concrete — depends on that specific material's density, which varies by type, moisture content, and source.

Compaction Can Affect Quantities

Loose material such as soil, mulch, or gravel can settle or compact after placement, which may mean more material is needed to reach a finished depth than the initial calculated volume suggests.

Supplier Measurements May Differ

Bulk material suppliers may measure, price, or round quantities differently than this calculator. Always confirm the exact quantity and pricing with your supplier before finalizing an order.

For important projects

For structural work, large-scale construction, or any project where getting the quantity wrong would be costly, confirm your material quantities with a qualified contractor, engineer, or your material supplier before purchasing.
